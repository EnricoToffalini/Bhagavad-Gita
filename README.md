# Bhagavad Gita

**Site: https://enricotoffalini.github.io/Bhagavad-Gita/**

The Sanskrit text with English and Italian translations, notes, glossaries, and free PDFs.

## The translation

The English and Italian versions were prepared from Google Translate output and
revised using GPT-5.4/5.5/5.6 and Claude Opus 4.8/5.0/5.5, along with some manual
editing. The aim was a translation that is readable, unbiased, and as literal as
possible.

## The text

The Bhagavad Gita is part of the Mahabharata (Book 6, the Bhishma Parva,
chapters 23–40). It is a dialogue set on a battlefield between Arjuna, who
hesitates to fight against his own relatives, and Krishna, an *avatar* of God.
Its theme is *dharma*: action in accordance with one's nature and the order of
things; the answer is *yoga*: discipline (of action, knowledge, and love), free
from attachment. It is rightly considered a synthesis and a masterpiece of
Indian philosophy.
